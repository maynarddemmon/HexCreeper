You are a single core on an endless grid. Grow it into a swarm of linked pieces, hold off the enemy squads, and explore for healing and score. The game ends when your core is destroyed.

<span style="font-size:16px;">YOUR TURN</span>
Each turn you may move as many pieces as your highest rank, each once, in any order. Click a piece to select it, then click a highlighted cell to move it, or use the keys Q E / A D / Z C. Tab cycles your pieces. End Turn (Enter) lets the enemies act; the turn ends by itself once nothing can move.

<span style="font-size:16px;">THE SWARM</span>
- Every piece you place links to a neighbour, its parent. Arrows point from each piece to its parent.
- Moving a piece carries everything linked below it, keeping its shape. Move the core to shift the whole swarm, or move a branch to reshape it.
- A linked piece must stay within 3 steps of its parent.
- If a piece is destroyed, everything linked below it goes too.
- Shift-click a cell held by one of your own pieces to move onto it instead of selecting it.

<span style="font-size:16px;">RANK</span>
A piece's rank is 1, or 1 + the lower of its two highest-ranked children, so a piece needs two children to rank up. Max HP is 2 × rank. Your highest rank sets your moves per turn; your core's rank sets the cost of Place and Heal (5 × rank).
A piece shows its rank as a symbol: rank 1 is a plain circle, 2 a line, 3 a triangle, 4 a cross, 5 a star, 6 three lines, 7 a seven-point star, 8 four lines, and from 9 a star with that many points.

<span style="font-size:16px;">ATTACKING</span>
Moving onto an enemy attacks it. When a group moves, every piece in it that would land on an enemy hits it. Killing n enemies in one move scores n², and pieces destroyed below a killed piece count too. After a kill, the attacker advances into the empty cell if it can.

<span style="font-size:16px;">ENEMIES</span>
Enemies arrive in waves of linked squads, and waves grow with your rank. A squad acts as often as its rank and plans its turn: it will carry a piece into reach, strike, and pull back out of reach. Kill a squad's leader to destroy the whole squad.

<span style="font-size:16px;">TILES</span>
- Green + heals the piece that steps on it; spare healing flows along links (up first, then down), and any left over becomes score. Its value, shown on the tile, is higher near unexplored quadrants.
- Red × deals damage to the first piece that moves onto it, then it's gone. Enemies walk around them.
- The trail of gold dots is the path your core has taken; it's darker where you've gone that way more than once.
- Grey hexes are solid: nothing can move onto them or see past them.
- Black hexes are holes: nothing can move onto them, but you can see across them.
- Dashed cells with a number are tiles counting down to appear. Quadrants you've visited are tinted blue.

<span style="font-size:16px;">FOG OF WAR</span>
You only see enemies within sight of your pieces: up to 12 steps away, with solid tiles blocking the view. Cells out of sight are dimmed. Tiles stay on the map once you've seen them. Fog of war can be turned off in Settings.

<span style="font-size:16px;">SPENDING SCORE</span>
- Place (P): pick an empty cell next to your pieces, then the piece to link it to.
- Heal (H): heal HP equal to your core's rank, starting from a piece you pick.

Save your game with the save button at the top right; it carries on from there the next time you open HexCreeper, and you can go back to it if your core is destroyed. The buttons next to it export your game to a file and import one. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom, Space to recenter, Esc to cancel. Settings has every number in the game.
